Brewing equipment

A brilliant brew starts with the right ritual.

Approachable tools for every kitchen, plus the tiny tips that make a real difference.

Tools for your brew

▱

French press

Rich, full-bodied coffee with a four-minute steep.

Tip: Use a coarse grind and pour slowly to avoid stirring up fines.

⌁

V60 dripper

A clean, bright cup with clarity in every tasting note.

Tip: Rinse the paper filter, then pour in gentle circles.

◒

AeroPress

Fast, portable and forgiving — perfect for one.

Tip: Start with 15g coffee, 230ml water and a one-minute brew.

◉

Hand grinder

Freshly ground coffee wherever you are.

Tip: Adjust finer for espresso and coarser for immersion brewing.
